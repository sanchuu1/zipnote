About ZipNote

ZipNote is a small company selling distinctive stationery: erasers shaped like cookies and pizza, pens that float, colouring books and pocket-sized toys.

What we sell

The range covers erasers, pens and sharpeners, notebooks and colouring books, toys and keychains, and a few gifts. Every product is priced between ₹40 and ₹199.
